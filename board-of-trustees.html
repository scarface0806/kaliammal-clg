About the Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees of Tmt. Kaliammal Palaniappan Educational & Charitable Trust is the apex governing body responsible for the overall direction, administration, and institutional welfare of Kaliammal College of Education. The Trust was founded in the year 2000 A.D. with a deep commitment to rendering meaningful educational and charitable service to the people of Tamil Nadu.

The Board provides strategic oversight for the college, ensuring that its operations remain aligned with the norms and standards prescribed by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), New Delhi, and the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai. The Trustees are collectively responsible for policy decisions, resource mobilisation, institutional development, and the long-term academic vision of the college.

The Board of Trustees meets periodically to review institutional progress, approve key policies, and deliberate on matters of academic quality, infrastructure, faculty welfare, and student support. Their dedicated service and philanthropic commitment have been instrumental in establishing and sustaining Kaliammal College of Education as a respected teacher education institution in the Karur district.

  • Established in 2000 with a mission of educational and social service
  • Founded Kaliammal College of Education in December 2005
  • Ensures full compliance with NCTE and TNTEU regulatory norms
  • Governs institutional policy, finance, and academic direction
  • Committed to accessible, high-quality teacher education in Tamil Nadu

Chairman & Correspondent

Shri. Aasee S Muthusamy

“Our commitment is to quality teacher education — nurturing dedicated, skilled educators who will build a stronger Tamil Nadu.”

Shri. Aasee S Muthusamy serves as the Chairman and Correspondent of Kaliammal College of Education, Karur. As Chairman, he provides the apex institutional leadership to the college, setting the strategic direction for academic growth, governance, and community engagement on behalf of the Board of Trustees.

In his dual role as Correspondent, Shri. Muthusamy serves as the principal institutional representative in all official dealings with the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU) and the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), ensuring full regulatory compliance and the smooth functioning of all institutional processes.

Under his leadership, Kaliammal College of Education continues to uphold its founding commitment to making quality teacher education accessible to deserving students from Karur and the surrounding districts, producing competent and values-driven teachers for the nation’s schools.

Governance Mandate

Policy & Vision

Formulating institutional policies, setting the long-term academic vision, and ensuring that all decisions align with the founding mission of the Trust.

Regulatory Compliance

Overseeing compliance with NCTE recognition norms, TNTEU affiliation requirements, and all applicable government regulations for teacher education institutions.

Resource Management

Approving budgets, managing financial resources, and directing capital investments in infrastructure, library, laboratories, and academic development.

Academic Welfare

Ensuring the welfare of faculty, staff, and students; upholding academic quality; and fostering an environment of integrity, inclusivity, and professional excellence.
