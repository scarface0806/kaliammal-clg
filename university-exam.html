Examination

University Examination

The university examination for students of Kaliammal College of Education is conducted by the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai. All B.Ed. and M.Ed. student-teachers registered with the college appear for the semester-end examinations organised by TNTEU at the end of each academic semester.

The college assists students in smooth completion of all examination formalities including registration, hall ticket collection, examination fee payment, and preparation guidance. Faculty members provide examination-oriented academic support to ensure every student-teacher is adequately prepared for university assessments.

  • Semester-end examinations conducted by TNTEU, Chennai
  • Hall tickets downloaded from TNTEU official portal
  • College facilitates timely examination registration and fee payment
  • Results published on TNTEU official website (www.tnteu.ac.in)
  • Re-examination and arrear paper facilities as per TNTEU rules

Examination Process

Key Steps & Information

Exam Registration

Students must register for the university examination through the TNTEU portal within the notified schedule. The college office assists in the registration process.

Hall Ticket

Hall tickets are issued by TNTEU and can be downloaded from the official university website. Students must carry the hall ticket to every examination session.

Results

Examination results are published by TNTEU on its official website. Students can access their individual mark sheets and grade cards through the university portal.

Certificates & Degrees

Degree certificates are issued by TNTEU upon successful completion of all programme requirements. The college facilitates the convocation registration process.
