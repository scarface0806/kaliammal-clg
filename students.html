Academics

Our Students

Kaliammal College of Education enrolls student-teachers from diverse educational, social, and regional backgrounds across Tamil Nadu. Both the B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes attract graduates with sincere aspirations for the teaching profession, and the college is committed to providing each student-teacher with a learning environment that nurtures academic excellence, professional competence, and personal integrity.

Admission to B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes is conducted through the centralised admission process prescribed by TNTEU and the Government of Tamil Nadu, ensuring transparent, merit-based selection. The college maintains an annual B.Ed. intake of 100 students and an M.Ed. intake as per NCTE norms.

  • B.Ed. annual intake: 100 seats (Tamil & English medium)
  • Admission through TNTEU centralised process
  • Active student council and academic clubs
  • Regular cultural, sports, and community activities
  • Scholarship and placement support for all eligible students

Student Life

Academic & Co-Curricular Engagement

Academic Activities

Seminars, debates, quizzes, and educational exhibitions that encourage research engagement, public speaking, and collaborative learning among student-teachers.

Cultural Events

Annual cultural programmes, Teachers’ Day celebrations, Republic Day and Independence Day events, and inter-college competitions fostering creativity and institutional pride.

Sports & Fitness

Sports activities, physical fitness programmes, and games that promote the holistic development, health, and team spirit of all student-teachers throughout the academic year.

Community Service

Social outreach programmes, awareness campaigns, and community engagement activities that instil a sense of social responsibility and civic consciousness in student-teachers.
