Courses & Admission

Master of Education (M.Ed.)

The Master of Education (M.Ed.) programme at Kaliammal College of Education is a two-year full-time postgraduate programme in teacher education. Designed for B.Ed. graduates seeking advanced expertise in educational theory, research, and administration, the programme is affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai, and recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). It develops research-oriented teacher educators, educational administrators, and curriculum specialists.

Programme At a Glance
Programme Master of Education (M.Ed.)
Duration 2 Years (Full-Time)
Annual Intake 25 Seats
Medium Tamil & English
Affiliation Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai
Recognition National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), Southern Regional Committee
Fee Structure As per TNTEU & Government norms (contact office for details)

What You Will Gain

Programme Highlights

The M.Ed. curriculum builds advanced competencies in educational research, policy analysis, curriculum design, and institutional leadership. It equips graduates for academic, administrative, and research careers in education.

  • Advanced study of educational philosophy, sociology, and policy
  • Research methodology, statistics, and educational measurement
  • Curriculum design, development, and critical evaluation
  • Educational administration, planning, and institutional management
  • Comparative education and global educational policy perspectives
  • Advanced educational psychology and learner development theories
  • Independent dissertation research on an approved educational topic
  • SWAYAM online learning modules as part of the curriculum

Statutory Approval

NCTE Recognition

The M.Ed. programme at Kaliammal College of Education is duly recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), Southern Regional Committee, Bangalore, as detailed below.

2015
Latest Recognition

Valid recognition granted by NCTE, Southern Regional Committee, Bangalore. This is the current operative recognition under which the M.Ed. programme is conducted.

2010
First Recognition

Initial recognition received from NCTE authorising the commencement of the M.Ed. programme, with an intake of 25 students per annum.

TNTEU
University Affiliation

The M.Ed. programme is affiliated to Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai, which prescribes the curriculum, conducts university examinations, and awards the degree.

Note: NCTE recognition documents are available for inspection at the college office and on the NCTE official portal as part of mandatory disclosure requirements.

Eligibility & Admission

Candidates holding a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) degree or equivalent teacher education qualification with a minimum of 55% aggregate marks (50% for reserved categories) are eligible to apply for the M.Ed. programme. Admission is conducted through the centralised counselling process as prescribed by the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU) and the Tamil Nadu government.

For enquiries regarding admission dates, fee structure, scholarship availability, and required documents, please contact the college office directly or visit Contact Us.
