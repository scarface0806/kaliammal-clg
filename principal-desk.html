From the Principal

Principal’s Message

“The Divine Tamil Sage ‘Thiruvalluvar’ eulogises the learned men by saying that they can become a native of any place and any country through his learning.”

"யாதானும் நாடாம் ஊராமால் என்னொருவன் சாந்துணையும் கல்லாத வாறு" – "The learned make each land their own, in every city find a home; Who, till they die, learn naught, along what weary ways they roam!" — Kural 397

The divine Tamil sage Thiruvalluvar eulogized the power of knowledge, observing that a learned person becomes a native of any land or country through their wisdom. This timeless truth is evidenced today by the global presence of scholars and professionals who transcend borders through their expertise.

Post-independent India has witnessed stupendous growth across various sectors, and education is no exception. The rapid expansion of educational institutions has served our nation by augmenting the human resources necessary to meet the government's development policies. However, we must remain vigilant against the growing trends of commercialization and commodification of education. It is essential for the public to discern and distinguish genuine learning centers from unworthy, pseudo-institutions.

Every nation defines its educational agenda based on its unique cultural heritage. Our National Policy on Education enshrines the constitutional goals of Socialism, Secularism, and Democracy.

The institutions established by the Tmt. Kaliammal Palaniappan Educational & Charitable Trust were founded to offer education in its purest sense, without diluting the noble goals envisioned by our Constitution. Our Trust is steadfast in observing the canons of honesty and integrity in its service to society. We ensure that every student entrusted to our care is groomed to become an exemplary citizen and a valuable asset to our country as we march toward a prosperous and powerful Bharat.
