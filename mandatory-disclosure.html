Transparency & Compliance

Mandatory Disclosure

In compliance with the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) Regulations and the guidelines of the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Kaliammal College of Education is required to publicly disclose essential institutional information pertaining to regulatory recognitions, accreditation status, annual surveys, and financial statements.

All information presented here is maintained in accordance with statutory norms applicable to teacher education institutions in India. Stakeholders, students, and regulatory authorities are welcome to refer to this page for official institutional disclosures.

Regulatory Compliance

Statutory Recognitions & Affiliation

A. Regulatory Recognition

National Council for Teacher Education

(NCTE)

The National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) is the statutory body established under the NCTE Act, 1993, responsible for the planned and coordinated development of the teacher education system. Kaliammal College of Education holds valid NCTE recognition for both the B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes.

Recognising Authority NCTE, Southern Regional Committee
B.Ed. First Recognition 2005
B.Ed. Latest Recognition 2015 (Renewed)
M.Ed. First Recognition 2010
M.Ed. Latest Recognition 2015 (Renewed)

Original recognition orders are available for inspection at the college office. For official documents, contact the Correspondent.

B. University Affiliation

Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University

(TNTEU)

Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU) is the affiliating university for all teacher education institutions in Tamil Nadu. Kaliammal College of Education is a duly affiliated institution of TNTEU, offering B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes under the university’s academic framework.

Affiliating University Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University
University Location Chennai, Tamil Nadu
Affiliated Programmes B.Ed. & M.Ed.
Affiliation Status Active & Current

Affiliation certificates and renewal orders are maintained at the college office and are available for verification by authorised parties.

Accreditation & Survey

Quality Assessment & National Survey

C. Accreditation

National Assessment & Accreditation Council

(NAAC)

The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) is an autonomous institution established by the University Grants Commission (UGC) to assess and accredit institutions of higher education in India. NAAC accreditation assures stakeholders of the quality standards maintained by the institution.

Accrediting Body NAAC, Bangalore
Accreditation Status As per latest records
Documents Available at college office

NAAC grading certificate and Self-Study Report (SSR) are maintained at the institution and available for inspection upon request.

D. National Survey

All India Survey on Higher Education

(AISHE)

The All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) is conducted annually by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, to collect data on all higher education institutions in the country. Kaliammal College of Education participates in this mandatory annual survey as required by government norms.

Conducting Authority Ministry of Education, GoI
Survey Frequency Annual
Participation Status Compliant – All Years

AISHE submission acknowledgements and data records are maintained at the administrative office for each academic year.

Financial Transparency

Financial Disclosures

E. Financial Statement

Balance Sheet

The Balance Sheet presents the financial position of the institution as at the end of each financial year, showing all assets, liabilities, and institutional reserves in accordance with applicable accounting standards.

  • Fixed Assets & Capital
  • Current Assets & Liabilities
  • Funds & Reserves

Available for inspection at the college office. Audited annually by a Chartered Accountant.

F. Financial Statement

Receipt & Payment

The Receipt and Payment Account records all cash and bank transactions of the institution for the financial year, providing a complete summary of receipts from all sources and payments to all parties.

  • Fee Collections & Grants
  • Salary & Administrative Payments
  • Capital Expenditures

Cash and bank transaction records are available for verification at the administrative office.

G. Financial Statement

Income & Expenditure

The Income and Expenditure Account presents the operating results of the institution for the financial year, reflecting all income earned and expenditure incurred in the course of running the institution’s programmes.

  • Tuition Fees & Other Income
  • Staff Salaries & Benefits
  • Infrastructure & Operations

Audited Income & Expenditure statements are maintained and available for inspection by authorised parties.

Statutory Note: All financial statements are prepared and audited in accordance with applicable accounting standards and submitted to the relevant regulatory authorities (NCTE, TNTEU) as part of the annual mandatory disclosure requirement. Physical copies are available for inspection at the college administrative office during working hours.
