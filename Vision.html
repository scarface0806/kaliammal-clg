Our Vision

Vision Statement

“To be a world-class, innovative, and highly acclaimed institution for Teacher Education. We are committed to fostering excellence and inclusivity by providing quality education to all sections of society, including minority communities and differently-abled students.”

Kaliammal College of Education envisions itself as a leading institution that shapes the character, competence, and commitment of teacher educators in Tamil Nadu. The college aspires to develop teachers who are not merely knowledge transmitters but true facilitators of holistic learning and growth.

The vision is rooted in the belief that education is the most powerful tool for individual and social transformation. By nurturing student-teachers who are reflective, research-oriented, and responsive to diverse learners, the institution aims to raise the standard of school education throughout the region.

Our Mission

Mission Statement & Objectives

To produce socially responsible, devoted, and dedicated teachers of high moral character and ethical standards. Our mission is to prepare educators who will inspire the future citizens of this nation to become principled and compassionate human beings.

The mission of Kaliammal College of Education is to provide structured, comprehensive, and value-based teacher education that equips student-teachers with the knowledge, skills, attitudes, and reflective competencies required to excel in diverse educational settings. The college is committed to fulfilling the national objectives of quality teacher preparation as mandated by NCTE and the affiliating university, TNTEU.

  1. To provide quality teacher education in strict alignment with the norms, standards, and regulations set by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) and Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU).
  2. To develop student-teachers’ academic competence, subject mastery, pedagogical skills, and capacity for reflective and critical thinking in real classroom contexts.
  3. To promote research, innovation, and the effective integration of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) into teaching and learning practices.
  4. To instil values of integrity, social responsibility, professional ethics, and a genuine commitment to inclusive and equitable education.
  5. To build practical teaching competencies through well-structured school internship programmes, collaborative lesson planning, and constructive supervision and feedback.
  6. To create an enriching and supportive learning environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, self-discipline, and a spirit of lifelong learning among student-teachers.
  7. To collaborate with schools, educational authorities, universities, and community organisations to continuously improve the quality and relevance of teacher education programmes.
  8. To empower student-teachers from all social and academic backgrounds with equal access to opportunities, resources, and academic guidance for professional and personal development.

Guiding Principles

Core Values

Excellence

Striving for academic excellence in teacher education through rigorous standards and continuous improvement.

Integrity

Upholding moral and ethical values in all academic, professional, and institutional activities.

Inclusivity

Creating equal opportunities for all student-teachers regardless of background, ability, or social circumstance.

Service

Dedicating institutional efforts to the service of learners, communities, and the broader educational ecosystem.
