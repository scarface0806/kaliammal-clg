Academics

Academic Profile

The Principal of Kaliammal College of Education is a senior academician with extensive experience in teacher education, educational research, and institutional administration. Holding advanced qualifications in Education, the Principal provides scholarly and administrative leadership across all academic functions of the college.

With a distinguished career in teacher education spanning more than two decades, the Principal has contributed significantly to curriculum design, faculty mentoring, and student development. The Principal actively guides research activities and fosters an environment of academic inquiry and professional growth within the institution.

Kaliammal College of Education continues to uphold its academic standards under the Principal’s leadership, maintaining full compliance with NCTE norms and TNTEU affiliation requirements while nurturing a generation of reflective and dedicated educators.

Academic Highlights

  • M.Phil. in Education — Advanced Specialisation
  • Master of Education (M.Ed.) — Pedagogical Foundation
  • Over two decades of experience in Teacher Education institutions
