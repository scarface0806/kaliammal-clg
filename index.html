What we provide

Kaliammal College of Education is committed to providing a holistic learning environment that equips every student-teacher with the academic knowledge, pedagogical skills, and personal values required to become a truly effective educator.

Infrastructure

Modern, well-equipped campus with smart classrooms, laboratories, library, sports facilities, and dedicated spaces for all academic activities.

Courses

NCTE-recognised B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes affiliated to TNTEU, offering structured two-year teacher education in Tamil and English medium.

Scholarships

Government scholarships for SC/ST, BC, MBC, and minority students. Institutional support ensures no eligible student is denied education due to financial constraints.

Placements

Strong placement record with graduates securing teaching positions in government and private schools across Tamil Nadu. Career guidance and placement support provided.

WELCOME TO Kaliammal College of Education

WHY CHOOSE US

Kaliammal College of Education stands as a premier teacher education institution in Karur, Tamil Nadu, offering NCTE-recognised B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes affiliated to Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU). With over two decades of commitment to educational excellence, the institution has nurtured hundreds of qualified, compassionate, and professionally competent teachers.

Our institution provides modern academic infrastructure, experienced faculty, structured practical training, and a value-based learning environment that prepares every student-teacher to contribute meaningfully to the future of school education in Tamil Nadu.

Correspondent’s Message

Heart felt Greetings. I extend a warm welcome to the viewers of our website. Tmt. Kaliammal Palaniappan Educational & Charitable Trust was founded in 2000 A.D. with the noble mission of rendering service to the Local Society. Hence our Trust with a firm conviction to offer qualitative educational service has started Teacher Education Courses in December 2005 in this Part of TamilNadu. The Blossoming of a Nation depends more upon the literacy rate of the masses than the other factors. Growth in literacy can be made a reality when the preceptors are of appreciable standards. Hence our Temple of Learning aims at the moulding of Teachers based upon the Norms and Standards prescribed by the National Council.

Principal’s Message

The Divine Tamil Sage ‘Thiruvalluvar’ eulogises the learned men by saying that they can become a native of any place and any country through his learning. This fact is testified by the presence of learned modern men living in cross border territories. The Post Independent India is witnessing stupendous growth in various fields. Education is not an exception. The explosive growth in the number of educational institutions has been serving the Nation in augmenting the Human resource appropriate to the requirements of the Development Polices of the Government. But we have to be cautious about the Trend of Commercialisation and Commodification of Education which has become an evil.
