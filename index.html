Chairman’s Message

Heart felt Greetings. I extend a warm welcome to the viewers of our website. Tmt. Kaliammal Palaniappan Educational & Charitable Trust was founded in 2000 A.D. with the noble mission of rendering service to the Local Society. Hence our Trust with a firm conviction to offer qualitative educational service has started Teacher Education Courses in December 2005 in this Part of TamilNadu. The Blossoming of a Nation depends more upon the literacy rate of the masses than the other factors. Growth in literacy can be made a reality when the preceptors are of appreciable standards. Hence our Temple of Learning aims at the moulding of Teachers based upon the Norms and Standards prescribed by the National Council.

Principal’s Message

The Divine Tamil Sage ‘Thiruvalluvar’ eulogises the learned men by saying that they can become a native of any place and any country through his learning. This fact is testified by the presence of learned modern men living in cross border territories. The Post Independent India is witnessing stupendous growth in various fields. Education is not an exception. The explosive growth in the number of educational institutions has been serving the Nation in augmenting the Human resource appropriate to the requirements of the Development Polices of the Government. But we have to be cautious about the Trend of Commercialisation and Commodification of Education which has become an evil.
