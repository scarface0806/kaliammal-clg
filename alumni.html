Our Alumni

Alumni Association

“Once a part of Kaliammal, always a part of Kaliammal — our alumni are our greatest legacy and our strongest ambassadors.”

The Kaliammal College of Education Alumni Association (KCEA) brings together all former students of the institution under a common platform of professional solidarity, institutional pride, and lifelong learning. Since the college’s establishment in 2005, hundreds of dedicated and qualified teacher-educators have graduated from our programmes and are now serving across Tamil Nadu and beyond.

Our alumni are not merely former students — they are the living representatives of the values, knowledge, and professional commitment that Kaliammal College of Education stands for. The Alumni Association serves as the enduring bridge between the institution and its graduates, fostering mutual support, professional development, and continued engagement with the alma mater.

We warmly invite all former students to register with the association, stay connected with their batchmates, and actively participate in the institution’s ongoing mission of excellence in teacher education.

Purpose

Association Objectives

  • To maintain a strong, active, and supportive network of former students of the institution.
  • To contribute to the continued growth, development, and reputation of Kaliammal College of Education.
  • To provide career guidance, mentorship, and professional support to current student-teachers.
  • To organise annual alumni meets, reunions, and academic interactions that strengthen institutional bonds.
  • To recognise and celebrate outstanding achievements of alumni in the field of education and public life.
  • To support scholarship and welfare programmes that benefit deserving students of the institution.
  • To organise community service activities that uphold the institution’s commitment to social responsibility.
  • To foster lifelong learning and professional development among all members of the alumni community.

Membership

All former students of Kaliammal College of Education who have completed the B.Ed. or M.Ed. programme are eligible to register as members of the Alumni Association.

Eligibility B.Ed. / M.Ed. Graduates
Membership Type Lifetime
Registration At College Office

For registration and enquiries, contact the Alumni Coordinator at the college office during working hours.

Our Initiatives

Key Alumni Activities

Annual Alumni Meet

A yearly gathering that reunites graduates across batches for interaction, networking, and institutional reconnection.

Career Guidance

Experienced alumni conduct sessions for current students on teaching career pathways, interview preparation, and professional growth.

Mentorship

Senior alumni volunteer as mentors, offering guidance on academic challenges, classroom management, and professional development.

Community Service

Alumni participate in community outreach, literacy drives, and educational initiatives aligned with the institution’s social mission.

Stay Connected: Alumni are encouraged to stay in touch with the institution, attend annual events, and contribute to the college’s ongoing development. For registration, event updates, and enquiries, please reach us at kaliammalcollege@gmail.com or call 6380496226.
