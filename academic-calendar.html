Academics

Academic Calendar

The academic calendar of Kaliammal College of Education is prepared at the beginning of each academic year in accordance with the guidelines and schedule prescribed by the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU) and the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE). The calendar outlines key academic events, examination schedules, internship periods, holidays, and co-curricular activities for the entire academic year.

All student-teachers and faculty are expected to follow the academic calendar strictly. Any changes to the schedule are communicated through official notices displayed on the college notice board and through the college office. For the current academic year’s calendar, please contact the college office.

Annual Schedule

Key Academic Events & Schedule

Period Academic Event
June – July Commencement of academic year; orientation programme; Semester I begins
August – September CIA I examinations; seminars and assignments; Independence Day celebrations
October – November CIA II examinations; school internship (Phase I); Teachers’ Day programme
November – December Semester I university examinations; semester break
January – February Semester II/III begins; school internship (Phase II); annual sports meet
March – April CIA examinations; practical examinations; annual cultural programme
April – May University examinations; result publication; academic year closure

Note: The above is an indicative academic schedule. Exact dates for each academic year are published by TNTEU and communicated through official college notices. Students must regularly check the notice board for updates.
