Courses & Admission

Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)

The Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) programme at Kaliammal College of Education is a two-year full-time teacher education programme designed to prepare student-teachers for effective classroom teaching at the secondary and higher secondary levels. Affiliated to the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai, and recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), the programme combines strong theoretical foundations with extensive practical school experience.

Programme At a Glance
Programme Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)
Duration 2 Years (Full-Time)
Annual Intake 100 Seats
Medium Tamil & English
Affiliation Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), Chennai
Recognition National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), Southern Regional Committee
Fee Structure As per TNTEU & Government norms (contact office for details)

What You Will Learn

Programme Highlights

The B.Ed. curriculum is carefully structured to develop well-rounded, reflective, and professionally competent teachers. Student-teachers gain both domain knowledge and the pedagogical skills needed to create meaningful learning experiences.

  • Preparation for secondary and higher secondary level classroom teaching
  • Comprehensive study of educational philosophy, psychology, and sociology
  • Specialised pedagogy in two chosen teaching methodology subjects
  • Micro-teaching, simulated teaching, and real classroom practice
  • School Internship of 20 weeks in affiliated practice teaching schools
  • Training in ICT integration, educational technology, and e-learning tools
  • Assessment, examination, and evaluation strategies for school contexts
  • SWAYAM online learning modules as part of the curriculum
Teaching Specialisations Offered

Statutory Approval

NCTE Recognition

The B.Ed. programme at Kaliammal College of Education is duly recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), Southern Regional Committee, Bangalore, as detailed below.

2015
Latest Recognition

Valid recognition granted by NCTE, Southern Regional Committee, Bangalore. This is the current operative recognition under which the programme is conducted.

2015
Continuation Recognition

Continuation of recognition issued by NCTE following compliance with prescribed norms, standards, and inspection requirements for teacher education institutions.

2005
First Recognition

Initial recognition received from NCTE in December 2005 at the time of establishment of the college, authorising the commencement of the B.Ed. programme.

Note: NCTE recognition documents are available for inspection at the college office and on the NCTE official portal as part of mandatory disclosure requirements.

Academic Structure

Curriculum Overview

Year I
  • Part I — Core Courses
  • Philosophical & Sociological Foundations of Education
  • Educational Psychology & Development
  • ICT in Education & Digital Learning
  • Part II — Pedagogy of Teaching
  • Pedagogy of Teaching — Method I
  • Pedagogy of Teaching — Method II
  • Part III — Practicum
  • Micro-teaching & Simulated Teaching Practice
Year II
  • Part I — Advanced Core Courses
  • Assessment, Evaluation & Examination Reforms
  • Educational Administration & Management
  • Guidance, Counselling & Special Education
  • Part II — Elective
  • Elective Course (as per TNTEU regulations)
  • Part III — School Internship
  • Block Teaching Practice (20 weeks in practice schools)
  • Part IV — Online Learning
  • SWAYAM Online Course (as per UGC/TNTEU guidelines)
Eligibility & Admission

Candidates holding a Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) in relevant subjects with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) are eligible to apply for the B.Ed. programme. Admission is conducted through the centralised counselling process as prescribed by the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU) and the Tamil Nadu government.

For enquiries regarding admission dates, fee structure, scholarship availability, and required documents, please contact the college office directly or visit Contact Us.
