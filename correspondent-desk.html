About the Correspondent

Correspondent’s Message

Heart felt Greetings. I extend a warm welcome to the viewers of our website. Tmt. Kaliammal Palaniappan Educational & Charitable Trust was founded in 2000 A.D. with the noble mission of rendering service to the Local Society. Hence our Trust with a firm conviction to offer qualitative educational service has started Teacher Education Courses in December 2005 in this part of Tamil Nadu.

The Blossoming of a Nation depends more upon the literacy rate of the masses than the other factors. Growth in literacy can be made a reality when the preceptors are of appreciable standards. Hence our Temple of Learning aims at the moulding of Teachers based upon the Norms and Standards prescribed by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE).

I take immense pride in the achievements of our student-teachers and the dedicated service of our faculty. The college strives to provide an environment where every aspiring educator receives the guidance, knowledge, and practical training required to become a truly effective teacher. I invite all stakeholders to work together towards this noble mission.

Institutional Leadership

Role of the Correspondent

The Correspondent is the designated management representative responsible for all statutory, administrative, and communicational responsibilities between the Trust and external educational authorities. The role involves ensuring full compliance with NCTE regulations, TNTEU affiliation requirements, and government norms applicable to teacher education institutions.

As the primary liaison with the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) and the Tamil Nadu Teachers Education University (TNTEU), the Correspondent oversees the renewal of recognitions, submission of mandatory disclosures, and maintenance of institutional records in accordance with statutory requirements.

  • Liaison with NCTE, TNTEU, and regulatory bodies
  • Oversight of admission processes and institutional policies
  • Ensuring compliance with mandatory disclosure norms
  • Coordination between Trust, faculty, and university
  • Governance and strategic direction of the institution
