National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE)

The National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) is a statutory body established under the National Council for Teacher Education Act, 1993, with the mandate to achieve planned and co-ordinated development of the teacher education system throughout India. The NCTE prescribes norms and standards for teacher education programmes, grants recognition to teacher education institutions, and ensures that standards of quality in teacher education are maintained.

Kaliammal College of Education is duly recognised by the Southern Regional Committee (SRC) of the NCTE, headquartered in Bangalore, for conducting both the Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) and the Master of Education (M.Ed.) programmes. This recognition authorises the institution to train teachers in accordance with the curriculum and norms prescribed by the NCTE.

Students and stakeholders may verify the NCTE recognition status of Kaliammal College of Education and download the recognition orders from the official NCTE website: www.ncte.gov.in. The college maintains all NCTE compliance requirements and cooperates fully with all regulatory inspections and recognition renewal processes.

Regulatory Compliance

NCTE Recognition & Norms

NCTE Recognition

The college holds valid NCTE recognition from the Southern Regional Committee (SRC) for B.Ed. and M.Ed. programmes with the approved annual student intake as per NCTE norms.

Infrastructure Norms

Compliance with NCTE-prescribed standards for land, built-up area, classrooms, laboratories, library, and other essential physical infrastructure for quality teacher education.

Faculty Norms

Maintenance of the required faculty qualifications, student-teacher ratios, and visiting faculty norms as stipulated by NCTE regulations for teacher education programmes.

Curriculum Compliance

Implementation of the NCTE-prescribed integrated B.Ed. curriculum framework, ensuring comprehensive theoretical study and professional practicum for all student-teachers.
